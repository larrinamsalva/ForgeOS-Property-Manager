# Milestone 4 — Utilities and billing

The merged Milestone 3 meter reader now feeds a supervised billing workflow. This release prepares draft invoices, approves an internal unissued balance, produces PDF/print statements and preserves customer ledger credits/reversals. No dedicated hosted database was configured or migrated, no deployment was performed, and no live customer invoice, message, payment or notice was created.

## Operator workflow

1. Open **Utilities & billing** and choose the property. An owner, administrator or assigned manager first authorizes its USD policy: actual calendar day proration, default planned due days, and explicit rent tax/authorization (zero is valid).
2. Add each property's dated electric/water/gas schedules. Enter the billed unit and exact conversion from every register unit, cumulative increasing tiers with one final unlimited tier, monthly base service, authorized water sewer pricing and any tax reference. Utilities included in rent use an explicit zero-price schedule. A replacement version closes the chosen prior schedule at its effective date. Unused mistakes can be voided with a reason; approved rates remain protected.
3. Collect and accept boundary readings in **Meters & reader**. Billing uses the latest accepted review and all accepted intermediate readings, retaining originals and corrections. A changed rate needs an approved reading on that effective civil date. Each assigned replacement meter needs its own baseline and closing readings. Missing boundaries, assignment gaps, unreviewed captures, unknown conversions and undocumented reset/rollover events block calculation.
4. For an accepted reset/rollover, use **Register events**. A reset requires documented register values immediately before/after the reset; a rollover requires its verified count. The event is tied to that accepted review. Unused incorrect events can be voided and replaced. Lower registers never become ordinary negative usage.
5. **New draft invoice** selects a recorded stay, service period and planned due date. Rent uses the agreed stay rate and effective recorded lease rates. Nightly, weekly and monthly terms are supported; seasonal/custom agreements require a manual authorized amount and reason. Optional fees and discounts retain their own reasons and tax authorization. The UI provides one fee and one discount; the validated API supports up to twenty adjustments.
6. Include utilities or explicitly omit them with a reason. Rent-only drafts are useful while waiting for readings; a later utilities-only invoice must omit rent with a reason. The current workflow includes all site utilities together. It does not selectively omit an individual meter or utility. Estimated accepted readings require an explicit acknowledgment.
7. Inspect the itemized draft and source evidence. **Refresh draft** appends a revision when contacts, policy, readings, rates or rent terms change. All earlier calculation snapshots remain visible. A supervisor's **Approve invoice** independently rebuilds and compares the saved calculation, refusing changed sources rather than repricing silently. Office staff may prepare/refresh drafts but cannot approve, alter configuration, credit or void.
8. Download PDF or open **Printable statement**. Every copy says draft, approved/not issued, or voided and states it is not a payment request. The customer ledger shows approved/unissued internal balances, with CSV export. A credit is limited to the invoice's remaining internal balance. A reversing entry exactly restores a credit once. Voiding retains the invoice and all ledger entries, reverses the remaining balance, and releases rent/usage claims for a corrected invoice.

## Calculation rules and limits

- Currency is explicitly USD. Financial totals never use binary floating point. PostgreSQL numeric performs consumption/pricing; bounded integer cents and decimal strings cross the API. The UI uses BigInt for totals and formatting.
- Each invoice line rounds half away from zero to cents; its tax rounds from the rounded line subtotal. Rent splits into separate lines at month and lease boundaries. Rational rent/base/sewer proration uses exact quotient/remainder rounding, so a half cent stays a half cent even with repeating daily fractions. A monthly fee applies actual covered days divided by that month's day count. Weekly rent uses covered days / 7; nightly rent uses covered days. Supported periods are within an actual stay and at most 366 days; planned occupancy alone is insufficient.
- Normal usage is closing minus baseline. Rollover usage is register capacity × verified count − baseline + closing. Reset usage is before-reset − baseline + closing − after-reset, with register bounds checked. Apply the meter multiplier and the configured conversion, then aggregate quantities per utility/rate version for tiers. Prices accept up to six decimal places. No rate-boundary consumption is interpolated.
- The latest accepted reading on each boundary date is used; a meter installed and removed within one civil date uses its earliest/latest approved observations. Captures must still fall within the assignment's exact instants. A rate change must have a reading on that date. A replacement at midnight may require arranging its final/initial captures and billing dates so those boundaries can actually be met; the engine blocks missing evidence rather than guessing.
- Tiers reset **per invoice and rate version**, aggregating all meters, including replacements. They are not shared across customer accounts or separate billing periods. Base service applies once per utility/rate version, with actual calendar day proration. Approved sewer combines water usage pricing and prorated sewer base into its own line. These conventions must be approved against the property's business rules before use.
- Drafts reserve rent dates and actual meter time intervals through database exclusion constraints. Splitting or extending an interval cannot bill it twice. Voiding releases claims, preserving every earlier source snapshot. Approved readings cannot receive a new review or changed event until all approved invoices using them are voided; a credit alone does not unlock their evidence.
- Later move-out/lease changes do not silently amend an approved statement. Reconcile through documented credit/void/correction workflows. Previous balances appear in the ledger, rather than being added to a new charge and billed twice.
- PDF statements embed pinned, licensed Noto Sans Latin fonts with pagination, item totals, rate/reading evidence and ledger history. Accented Spanish names are verified. Unsupported glyphs return a clear 422 response and direct the operator to browser printing, preserving the original customer name. The browser statement supports the browser's Unicode fonts. There is no external runtime font request.
- Invoice statuses in this milestone are draft, approved/unissued and voided. Issuance, email, payment collection/allocation, receipts, deposits, paid/overdue states, grace/late-fee rules and collections remain later work.

## Database and authorization

The CLI-created additive migration is `supabase/migrations/20261021191645_utilities_billing.sql`; all three prior migrations and the MIT license remain unchanged. The new public tables are property policy versions, utility rate schedules, register events, invoice headers, immutable invoice revisions and immutable ledger entries. Private tables retain retry requests, invoice counters and rent/usage claims. All 25 public application tables and seven private tables have RLS and explicit grants. Financial writes run through an authenticated invoker wrapper and narrowly scoped private definer functions, with empty search paths, current identity/role checks and property-first locks. The calculation helper is not executable by application roles. Changes are audited with the original caller identity.

Financial rows are available only to owner/admin and assigned manager/office roles. Meter technicians and maintenance receive no financial rows, even by direct API requests. Cross-property/organization IDs, anonymous statements, forged actor/status fields and cross-origin mutations are rejected. PDF and print routes read the header, stored revision and ledger in one consistent database statement snapshot through the ordinary session/RLS and send private/no-store responses. No finance records enter the offline reader, service worker or browser storage. Per-operation UUIDs compare exact actor/payload content before accepting retries.

## Verification

The story under test is: approved meter observation → billing UI → validated API → authenticated PostgreSQL calculation/claims → saved revision → supervisor approval → internal ledger → PDF/print → credit/reversal/void.

Local verification uses fictional records and the actual migrations in embedded PostgreSQL with `btree_gist`. The loopback HTTP fixture supplies deterministic Auth/REST/private Storage boundaries; it is not hosted Supabase Auth, PostgREST or Storage and must never be deployed. The historical baseline helper simulates the route creation clock only; both captures, reviews and all financial operations use the production transactional functions.

The unit/database suite covers exact decimal formatting, strict input/status validation, leap/calendar proration, nightly/weekly/monthly/custom and changed lease rent, exact half cents, tier/multiplier/tax arithmetic, water/gas conversions, sewer/included utilities, replacements, reset/rollover, estimated sources, missing boundaries/conversions, dated rate splits, overlapping rent/usage, idempotency, stale approval/refresh, protected readings, credit limits/reversal/void, grants and RLS. Browser checks cover capture/review through invoice/ledger, duplicate retries, PDF download, printable statements, export, anonymous/cross-origin/role boundaries, accessible forms, both themes and three viewports. Earlier property/planning/offline flows remain regressions. PDF pages are rendered and visually inspected for pagination, text, columns and footers.

Local results: **70 unit/database tests in nine files passed**. The full browser suite passed **25 checks**, with **14 intentional duplicate lifecycle skips** across viewports. The final statement-snapshot/PDF changes were also verified through the three desktop billing browser scenarios. ESLint, TypeScript and the optimized production build passed. The production PDF route trace contains both embedded font files.
