# Milestone 5 — Payments and collections

ForgeOS now records manual payments and refunds, allocates funds to explicitly issued invoices, keeps deposits as a separate liability, generates receipts, and supports aging, manual late fee review, arrangements, follow-ups and statement reconciliation. This builds on merged Milestone 4. No hosted database migration, deployment, payment processor activation, customer message or real financial transaction was performed.

## Operator workflow

1. An organization owner or administrator opens **Payments & collections → Collections policy** for the property. Explicitly authorize manual issuance, grace days, optional manually reviewed late fees/tax/cap, and optional deposit deductions. Record the contract/jurisdiction review reference and reason. Defaults are disabled; every change appends a policy version. Issued invoices retain their actual due date and grace version.
2. Prepare and approve charges in **Utilities & billing**. Approval alone remains unissued. In **Invoices & aging**, **Issue** records the actual issue/due dates against the approved revision. No message is sent. Manual financial dates must be on/after 2000-01-01. Issue date may be historical for an authorized manual record, but never future; due must be on/after issue and within 366 days. Wrong issuance dates require reversing allocations, voiding the invoice, and preparing a documented replacement.
3. **Payments → Record payment** records funds already received through the property's existing process, creates a numbered receipt, and leaves the amount unapplied. Methods are cash, check, bank transfer, card processed elsewhere (reference only), and other. Noncash requires an external reference; duplicate active references at the property/method are blocked. Never enter card or bank account numbers. Allocations may split one receipt across invoices or combine several receipts on one invoice, within available funds and the same customer/property. Only approved, explicitly issued invoices accept allocations.
4. Managers record refunds of unapplied funds using the actual payout method and an external reference when noncash. A refund records a completed external action; ForgeOS sends no payout. Reverse an allocation before refunding its allocated funds. **Reverse event** creates an exact linked inverse; each original may be reversed once. Void a mistaken payment only after all allocations/refunds are reversed. Record its replacement with **Corrects voided payment**. Original records and receipts remain intact.
5. **Deposits → Open deposit account** creates a security or reservation liability account, optionally linked to a matching stay or reservation. Record received funds separately. Stay/reservation deposit notes are references and are deliberately not imported as money. Managers can return held funds, apply them to an issued invoice, or make an explicitly configured deduction against a separate issued fee-only invoice. Applying/deducting reduces the liability and invoice balance without creating another cash receipt or billing income again. Every movement/reversal has a receipt. Actions cannot make held funds negative or overpay an invoice.
6. **Receipts** offers PDF and printable records for payments, refunds, voids, refund reversals, and every deposit event. Sequential `RCT-` numbers are unique per property. Property/customer details and original source evidence are frozen at creation. The original payment receipt additionally shows current allocation/refund/correction history; voided or reversed originals are clearly marked. Receipt amounts identify the event, rather than treating every receipt as new income. PDFs embed pinned Noto Sans Latin fonts, wrap long references and paginate. Unsupported characters return 422 with a browser-print fallback that preserves the name.
7. **Invoices & aging** shows current, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90 and over-90-day outstanding amounts using the property's local date and actual due date. Grace independently controls overdue status and fee eligibility. Unissued approvals, drafts, paid and voided invoices do not enter aging. An invoice can be draft, approved/unissued, issued, partially paid, paid, overdue or voided in the collection view. Overdue takes precedence over partially paid when an unpaid balance remains after grace. Aging is current, not a historical “as of” accounting report. Export includes integer cents and neutralizes spreadsheet formulas.
8. After grace, **Prepare late fee** computes a separate fee-only draft using the current owner policy: `min(cap, flat + round(outstanding × basis_points / 10000))`, then the configured line tax. Rounding is exact half away from zero. Managers must review/approve that draft in Billing and explicitly issue it in Collections. Approval rejects a changed balance or policy; void/reassess a stale fee. One active assessment is allowed per source invoice; no fee on another late fee. **Record waiver** posts a retained ledger credit, bounded by the fee invoice's outstanding balance. Billing's existing exact credit reversal remains available. Voiding a source invoice requires first resolving its linked fee invoices and payments.
9. **Notes & follow-ups** records internal notes, dated manual follow-ups and manager-authorized payment arrangements on issued invoices. A promise does not reduce debt. A follow-up becomes due on its date, or when the promise date arrives, and remains unresolved until a linked resolution is added. Arrangements have one promised amount/date; this milestone does not implement installment schedules or automated reminders. Contact customers through the property's authorized existing process.
10. **Reconciliation** covers the selected property, regardless of the customer filter. Select up to 1,000 unmatched cash movements from an external statement/journal; specify method, inclusive start/exclusive end, opening/closing balances and statement reference. Preview shows exact expected closing and variance before the separate **Record reviewed reconciliation** action. Payments/deposit receipts add cash; refunds, returns and void corrections subtract it; their reversals invert it. Allocations and deposit applications are excluded. Record a nonzero variance only with an owner's explicit acknowledgment/explanation; it remains an unresolved discrepancy, without a fabricated balancing entry. This is manual matching, not a connected bank feed or confirmation that funds settled.

## Reconciliation and correction rules

A source belongs to only one active reviewed batch. Overlapping active periods for the same method, or an all-method period, are rejected. Adjacent periods of the same method must carry forward the prior closing balance. Older unmatched movements can be selected in a later period and are counted as carry-forwards. Later-entered historical receipts remain unmatched and do not rewrite an earlier reviewed snapshot. New correction events are separate cash movements, even when the original is already reviewed. This is not a period-close lock or a complete general ledger.

An owner may void a reviewed batch with a reason, retaining its snapshot and releasing its selected sources. A following adjacent period of the same method must be voided first. Events, receipts, original batches, notes and policy versions cannot be edited or deleted by the application. The workflow does not erase a paid receipt to “fix” a balance. Backdated payment/deposit events cannot overdraw a daily balance by borrowing from a later receipt or reversal.

## Authorization and data model

The CLI-created additive migration is `supabase/migrations/20261021201615_payments_collections.sql`. Earlier migrations and MIT license remain unchanged. All **37 public application tables and 11 private tables** have RLS and explicit grants. The twelve new public tables contain policy versions, invoice issuance, manual payments, payment events, deposit accounts/events, receipts, fee assessments/waivers, notes, reconciliation batches and reconciliation voids. Four private tables hold exact retry requests, receipt counters and active reconciliation/fee claims.

| Role                                                  | Financial read              | Authorized writes                                                                                                                                |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Owner / administrator                                 | All organization properties | All manual actions; policy authorization, variance acknowledgment and reconciliation voids                                                       |
| Assigned manager                                      | Assigned property           | Issue invoices, receive/allocate/refund/reverse/void payments, deposit actions, fee review/waiver, arrangements and zero-variance reconciliation |
| Assigned office                                       | Assigned property           | Receive/allocate payments, open deposit accounts, receive deposits, ordinary notes and their resolutions                                         |
| Maintenance / meter technician / resident / anonymous | None                        | None                                                                                                                                             |

Archived customers can settle historical accounts; the property itself must be active and restored before new financial actions. Current database membership is checked on every transaction. A property-first lock serializes competing financial mutations, while source claims, unique reversal links, amount checks and composite scope keys protect the records. Credits are now bounded by outstanding amounts after payment/deposit allocations; a paid invoice cannot be voided until allocations are reversed.

The public command functions are invoker wrappers. Narrow private definer functions use empty search paths, explicit identity/role checks, no application raw write grants and audited caller identity. Actor-bound operation UUIDs compare exact payloads before accepting retries. Cross-property/customer links, forged fields and cross-origin mutations are rejected. `/api/collections` validates strict JSON with a 128 KiB limit under the ordinary authenticated session. Monetary values are bounded integer cents or exact decimal strings; client sums use BigInt and SQL sums use numeric. No service-role key is used.

Atomic SQL snapshots return collection balances/status, a receipt with its current history, and an invoice with its revision, ledger and collection history. Screens use these balances for display and the transaction rechecks them when saving. Authenticated HTML, JSON and PDF responses are private/no-store. Financial data never enters the tablet offline cache or browser storage.

## Optional payment architecture

`src/lib/payment-processing.ts` declares separate tenant collection and ForgeOS subscription adapter contracts. Both selectors return `null`. There is no checkout implementation, processor SDK, key, merchant account, webhook route, subscription charge, raw card form or activated payment flow. A manually recorded external-card reference is evidence entered by staff, not processor verification.

Before a future adapter is activated, select and authorize the processor/account and funds flow, validate tenant/property/invoice ownership and live outstanding amounts, use hosted payment collection, verify webhook signatures against raw bytes, enforce provider-event and request idempotency, preserve settlement/refund/reversal evidence, and keep SaaS subscription money outside tenant financial records. Apply provider-specific requirements and test them in an isolated sandbox. This release deliberately provisions no external service.
