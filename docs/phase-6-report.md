# Milestone 6 — Maintenance, inventory and daily operations

This release adds property operations to the merged Milestones 1–5. It uses the existing ordinary Supabase session, scoped database roles and reviewed PR workflow. The original repository address in the master build prompt is correct, and the MIT license and earlier migrations remain unchanged.

## Operator workflow

1. An employee registers their own account. The owner grants the actual manager, office or maintenance property role in **Team & access**. A supervisor adds the registered employee's profile in **Team & daily operations → Staff & history**, including employee code, contact, start date and supervisor. The profile displays current database roles; it cannot grant or change them. An inactive profile blocks assigned operations actions, but does not revoke other application permissions. Use Team & access for that revocation.
2. In **Maintenance**, office staff create a request with property, optional site/customer, problem, category, priority, requested/due date, parts required and assignment. An unassigned request starts New; an assigned request is Assigned or Scheduled depending on its due date. Work can move through In progress, Waiting parts and Completed. A supervisor approves and closes completed work. Reopening appends history; a previously closed order requires a supervisor to reopen.
3. The assigned employee records notes and time, uses or partially returns materials, uploads request/before/after images, checks out equipment for the job, returns it with condition and marks work complete. Each event retains actor and timestamp. Completion notifies authorized managers in the app. Office staff can record estimates, actual expenses and exact expense reversals before closure. Actual cost includes net materials and recorded expenses. Recorded time is not automatically priced, and no expense creates an invoice, supplier payment or tenant charge.
4. In **Inventory & purchasing**, supervisors maintain SKU, unit, storage location, supplier, standard unit cost, reorder/restock quantities and notes. Office staff record receipts; supervisors record signed adjustments or matching SKU/unit transfers between two authorized properties. Stock history, last purchase date and low-stock alerts are derived from retained movements. An adjustment with a negative quantity records removal not linked to a work order.
5. Employees request parts for their assigned active jobs; office staff can request general restock. Supervisors approve requests. Office staff prepare immutable supplier/line snapshots and supervisors approve the order, then record that it was placed through the existing supplier process. Orders have up to 30 distinct items and can link approved requests with the same item and quantity. Office staff receive partial deliveries; supervisors record partial returns against individual receipts. A returned delivery can reopen the remaining quantity for replacement. The order list derives Ordered, Part received or Received from net receipts. An order can be cancelled only after all received quantity is returned. Cancelling releases its request claims while retaining history.
6. Supervisors maintain equipment identification, serial, condition, next service date and repair history. Available good/fair equipment with no overdue service can be checked out once. Employees check out to themselves for an authorized job and return their own loan; office staff manage loans within their property. Returned equipment can be marked as needing repair. Supervisors record service, optionally linking a completed repair order, and schedule the next service. Active checkout prevents retirement, archive and service.
7. Supervisors create preventive work plans or custom daily checklist plans. Plans repeat at an interval of 1–366 calendar days from an anchor date, optionally ending on a date. The calendar shows expected occurrences and existing generated records. Office staff explicitly create an occurrence. Each plan/date generates exactly one retained work order or checklist run, including after cancellation. Editing a plan affects future generation; generated work retains a plan snapshot and generated checklist items remain frozen.
8. Employees record Done or Skipped with a reason on assigned, due checklist items. A run closes only after every item has a recorded done/skip outcome. Future runs cannot be completed early. Office staff can reopen items/runs or cancel runs. Entries append rather than overwrite earlier outcomes. Assigned work, completion review and checklist assignments create private in-app notifications; marking one read affects only its recipient.

All operations screens require a connection. Forms preserve errors and use stable request IDs while retrying an action. Photo failures retain pending metadata; select the same original file to retry. Operations photos and drafts do not have the tablet meter reader's encrypted offline recovery. No automatic job generation or outbound message delivery runs.

## Permission boundaries

| Role                               | Operations access                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Organization owner / administrator | Supervisor access across the organization's authorized properties.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Property manager                   | Full operations within assigned properties; catalog/profile/plan configuration, adjustments/transfers, purchasing decisions, work approval and service. A transfer needs supervisor access at both properties.                                                                                                                      |
| Office                             | Read operational records/costs, create/assign work, record expenses/receipts, prepare orders, generate occurrences, manage checklist runs and equipment loans. Cannot approve/close completed work, adjust/transfer stock, approve/place/cancel purchase orders, return purchase receipts or change staff/catalog/plan definitions. |
| Active maintenance employee        | Own assigned work and checklists; limited site labels, sanitized part/equipment availability, job notes/time/materials/photos and own authorized equipment checkout/return. No customer contacts, supplier records/costs, inventory movements, purchase-order costs, billing or collections.                                        |
| Meter technician                   | Existing assigned meter routes only; no operations work or stock access from this role.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Resident / anonymous               | No operations records or private objects.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |

A staff profile alone supplies no role. Permissions use current database membership on each request. Assignment to a different employee, role revocation or inactive employment blocks subsequent assigned access. Menu visibility is supplementary; RLS and command checks enforce the boundaries directly. Notifications require both recipient identity and current property operations access. Audit snapshots remain owner/administrator-only.

## Data and transactions

The additive migration `20261022003037_property_operations.sql` creates 20 public and four private tables. There are now **57 public application tables and 15 private tables**, all with RLS and explicit grants. Earlier migrations are unchanged.

| Public records                                                                                      | Purpose                                                                                                                               |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `staff_profiles`, `suppliers`, `inventory_items`                                                    | Actual-account staff metadata and versioned property catalogs.                                                                        |
| `inventory_movements`                                                                               | Immutable signed receipts, adjustments, uses/returns and linked transfers.                                                            |
| `work_orders`, `work_order_events`, `work_order_costs`, `work_order_materials`, `work_order_photos` | Request lifecycle, approval/time history, separate office-only cost history, sanitized material snapshots and private photo metadata. |
| `operations_plans`, `checklist_runs`, `checklist_entries`                                           | Recurrence definitions, frozen assigned runs and append-only item outcomes.                                                           |
| `equipment`, `equipment_loans`, `equipment_events`                                                  | Current identity/condition, retained checkouts/returns and service history.                                                           |
| `purchase_requests`, `purchase_orders`, `purchase_order_lines`, `purchase_receipts`                 | Reviewed requests/orders, frozen supplier/items/prices and signed delivery/return history.                                            |
| `operations_notifications`                                                                          | Recipient-private assignment/completion notices and read status.                                                                      |

Private tables retain actor-bound exact request payloads/results, per-property WO/PO counters, unique plan/date occurrence claims and active request/order claims. The private schema stays unexposed. Authenticated sessions have SELECT grants on application tables and call narrow public invoker wrappers for authorized transactions. Private elevated functions have empty search paths, explicit authentication/scope checks and no generic table-writing interface.

`/api/operations` validates the current user, same-origin JSON commands, strict allowlisted Zod payloads and bounded values. It uses the caller's ordinary publishable-key session. The read endpoint loads stable pages of authorized records, exact-text aggregates and explicitly sanitized context. No service-role key is used. Authenticated results are private/no-store. Errors produce unavailable states rather than fabricated empty data.

Every mutation locks the active property before dependent records. Transfers lock both property UUIDs in sorted order. This serializes competing stock, loan and occurrence changes and prevents archive/write races. Unique indexes independently enforce one active equipment loan, one SKU per property and active occurrence/request claims. Configuration, work/checklist status and purchase decisions require the last-read version, rejecting stale forms. Successful retries with the same UUID, actor, command and exact payload return the original result; changed content conflicts.

Quantities are integer milliunits with up to three user-facing decimals. Each movement is bounded to 999,999,999.999 units. Money is integer USD cents and remains decimal text in JSON; quantities and aggregate money use exact `numeric`/`BigInt` arithmetic. Per-event monetary values are bounded to 999,999,999,999 cents. No binary floating-point dollars or quantities are used for stock/cost arithmetic.

Each stock event checks both current quantity and the cumulative daily timeline; a backdated removal cannot create a negative historical day. Work issues and transfers use the item's standard unit cost at that event and freeze the amount. Purchase receipts use the frozen order line price. Cumulative proportional rounding allocates each partial material return and purchase receipt/return against its original total so the final reversal matches exactly, including fractional-cent sources. Transfers append equal/opposite linked movements atomically. This is operational cost tracking, without FIFO, weighted-average accounting, taxes, landed costs, depreciation or general-ledger valuation.

Manual expenses reverse through a unique linked inverse, and estimate changes append history. Equipment returns update the retained loan and append a condition event; changing its catalog also appends an equipment event and audit snapshot. Staff, catalogs, plans, job and checklist lifecycle updates retain audit snapshots. No application delete is granted. SKU/unit changes after stock history are refused. Stock archive requires zero quantity and no outstanding purchase line quantity.

## Private photo evidence

`work-photos` is private, with a 5 MB limit and JPEG, PNG and WebP allowlist. Metadata reserves an organization/property/actor/photo path before upload. The upload route checks the authenticated actor, assignment, declared and actual size, signature and SHA-256, then writes with upsert disabled. An already-uploaded identical file can finish an interrupted completion step. The final database function checks object metadata and current active-property/job access before marking it uploaded.

Authorized viewers receive a 60-second signed URL. Pending objects can be read only by their authorized creator to recover completion; uploaded objects require work access. Supervisors void mistaken photos with a retained reason; metadata and objects remain, but future signed access is denied. Raw object update/delete is not granted. As with any signed URL, an already issued URL may remain usable until its short expiry. Hosted Storage behavior must be verified separately.
